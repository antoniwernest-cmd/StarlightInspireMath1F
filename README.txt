STARLIGHT 1 | WEEK 1 NUMBER SENSE AND OPERATIONS

Open index.html. This is a separate, one-week edition of Starlight for
Monday, October 5 through Friday, October 9, 2026. It has Pathway Star
(Grade 1), Excite Star (Grade 2), and Inspire Star (Grade 3), each with
Home and School routes. The original four-week Starlight is unchanged.

Week 1 has 1,121 questions in all, including assigned work and optional
practice. Every day is one continuous, printable worksheet. School
entry uses teacher code 300. Teacher fill uses code 259. Assessment
and question-bank PDFs are available without a password and are named
Starlight_1 to keep them distinct from the original project's PDFs.
Answers, reflections, and school-unlock state use separate browser keys
so this edition does not overwrite the original project's records.

Block-trading place-value expressions show their movable base-ten blocks,
not an unrelated jump line. All genuine addition and subtraction tasks
have an open number line alongside their main manipulatives. Two-team
comparisons show both lines at once. Rounding, estimation-after-counting,
ordering arithmetic cards, means, and book-graph totals also have
appropriate number-line models. For subtraction, the starting number
is labelled at the right; students jump left to count back. Addition
starts on the left and moves right. Block-bank pieces and two-group
addition pieces retain drag-and-drop as well as tap controls. A student's
number-line jumps are included in the PDF assessment when used.
Jump-size buttons stay visible after a drag. Excite and Inspire show
50 and 100 jumps for addition and subtraction; Pathway stays within 50.
Large jumps can pass the goal when they remain inside the grade's number
range, and the highlighted return jump compensates. For example, 178 - 19
can be shown as -10, -10, +1, ending at 159. On 160 - 12, students may
use -50 then +38, or -100 then +88. All jumps are saved in the PDF.

On block-building and other hands-on arithmetic pages, the jump line spans
the full worksheet width below the manipulatives. Tablet pull-to-refresh
and long-press menus on non-editable project content are suppressed across
the landing, grade, and day pages. Text-entry fields remain editable.
On wide screens, the worksheet uses the available screen width rather than
staying in a narrow centred strip; block-bank and model pieces grow too.
Question titles show a strategy emoji, and story cards show a matching
context emoji. Two-team comparisons show jersey badges beside both teams
on their number lines, answer choices, and comparison results.
Once estimation counts are revealed, their number lines name both groups
(for example, Box A 44 + Box B 44 = ?) and show the required jump amount,
the amount still left to add, and only jumps that fit the remaining goal.
Choosing an own jump adds a reusable "My jump" button to that question's
number line. In two-team questions, each line keeps its own choices. A saved
jump stays visible but is disabled if it exceeds the amount left to jump.
Every question header and PDF question entry shows its Ontario code with
a brief description of the skill; the full expectation stays in the
curriculum view and assessment introduction.

Excite (Grade 2) includes 13 assigned two-digit + two-digit addition
problems across the five Home days and five School days. Their totals are
at most 100, consistent with B2.4. No three-digit + three-digit problem
was added: two three-digit whole numbers cannot have a sum below 200,
and the only sum equal to 200 (100 + 100) exceeds B2.4's operation range.
Numbers up to 200 remain in B1.1 place-value and B1.4 counting work.
The verification script audits every Grade 1 and Grade 2 B2.4 operation
against its grade limit and checks the new Grade 2 additions and answers.
